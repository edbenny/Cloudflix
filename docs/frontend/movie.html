Overview

Paul Atreides unites with Chani and the Fremen while on a warpath of revenge against the conspirators who destroyed his family. Facing a choice between the love of his life and the fate of the known universe, he endeavors to prevent a terrible future only he can foresee. As Paul is drawn deeper into the politics and mysticism of the Fremen people, he must decide who he truly is — and what he is willing to sacrifice to protect the world he has come to call home.
